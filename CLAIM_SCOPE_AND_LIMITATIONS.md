# Scope and limitations

For each infinite field the logarithm of the number of principally regular symmetric-matrix conditional-independence patterns is Θ(n³). The same estimate holds over each ordered field with all principal minors positive.

Labels are fixed. Each field has its own count. The proof also constructs rational positive definite realizations arbitrarily near the identity for a subfamily. The proportion of patterns admitting these realizations (Question 7.5) remains open.

No earlier superquadratic lower bound for these same patterns surfaced. The qualifying contribution is the cubic lower bound over each infinite field, including positive realizations over each ordered field. Confidence in the priority search is moderate.
