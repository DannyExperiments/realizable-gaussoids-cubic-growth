There are two labels in each of A, B and K. The two selectors k₁ and k₂ have weights 1 and 2, so their subsets have distinct sums 0, 1, 2 and 3.

With t = 1/16 and the K block equal to the identity, the almost-principal determinant for (aᵢ, bⱼ | S) equals t² × (stored sum − tested sum). The table evaluates the minors of the rational matrix exactly.

Each of the four pairs stores two bits independently. There are 2⁸ = 256 patterns in this six-variable construction. In general there are |A| × |B| pairs and |K| bits per pair, giving |A| × |B| × |K| bits, cubic when the three blocks have comparable sizes.
